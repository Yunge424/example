What Leeds FAN does

Bringing local food support into one clearer network

Leeds Food Aid Network exists to bring together the people and organisations working to tackle food poverty in Leeds. The network helps food aid providers, agency workers, Leeds City Council and the wider public connect more effectively.

Food aid includes food banks, food drop-ins, street outreach, food pantries, social enterprise projects and other forms of support for people experiencing food poverty.

01

Signposting

People and services refer residents to appropriate food aid providers.

02

Distribution

Food suppliers, supermarkets, shops and individuals help distribute food to providers.

03

Longer-Term Assistance

Food providers connect people with services that can help with wider needs.
